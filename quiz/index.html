Before you start

How it works. Eighteen questions, three answers each. Pick the one that's actually true, not the one that sounds best — no answer here is the wrong answer, and nothing is being scored as good or bad behaviour.

What you get. Two numbers, a spot on a grid, and the name of the friendship role your habits most resemble. Then the part that matters: whether researchers have ever studied young people using AI that way. For some of the roles on this page, the honest answer is no.

What this is not. Not a diagnosis, not a personality type, not a verdict on you. It describes a pattern of use, and patterns change.
